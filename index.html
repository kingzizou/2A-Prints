Withholding tax that follows the Ghana rules

Pick what each supplier supplies and the program does the rest: 3% on goods, 5% on works and 7.5% on services once a supplier passes GHS 2,000 in the year, and 20% for non-residents. It is worked out before VAT, on payments and on cash purchases.

    Before you buy

    Can I try it first?

    Yes. Download it or open the web app, create your company and use it on the free 30-day trial. When you are ready, enter your licence key and carry on with the same data.

    How do I get a licence key?

    Message us your company name, the number of users and the period you want. We confirm the price, you pay by Mobile Money, and once the payment is confirmed your key is sent to your email. In the app, open the licence screen and paste it in. A key belongs to one company and lasts for the period you paid for.

    Is my business data kept separate from other companies?

    Yes. Each company's records are stored separately and only people you invite can open them.

    Does it handle NHIL, GETFund, VAT and withholding tax?

    Yes. Rates are set once in the admin area and applied to sales, purchases and expenses. Withholding tax follows the Ghana rules on supplier payments, cash purchases and cash expenses, and the tax summary reports VAT, NHIL and GETFund.

    Windows says it protected my PC. Is the app safe?

    Windows shows this notice for any new program that has not built up a download history yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Your files are not touched, and your company data is kept in the cloud under your own login.

    Is there an Android app?

    Yes. Download it above and install it on your phone or tablet. It opens the same company books as your computer, prints and shares invoices, and offers new versions by itself. On an iPhone, open the web app and add it to your home screen.

    What happens if the internet goes off?

    You can still open your records and keep selling with Quick Sale. Those sales are kept safely on the device and sent to the cloud as soon as the connection is back. How long offline selling is allowed is set by your administrator.

    Can I add more people later?

    Yes. Ask us to add users and you pay only the difference for the time left on your licence. Each person gets a role that decides which screens they can use.

    Can I move over from Excel?

    Yes. Add your customers, suppliers, products and opening balances, and carry on from there. Ask us if you want help setting up.
